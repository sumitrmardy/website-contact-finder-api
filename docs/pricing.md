# Pricing Model

The [Website Contact Finder](https://apify.com/sumitr_mardy/website-contact-finder) charges per discovered contact event:

- **`contact_found`**: $0.006 per domain where an email or phone number is found (down to $0.003 on Gold tier, from $3.00 per 1k).
- **Free non-hits**: Domains that are dead, blocked, unreachable, or return zero contacts are completely free.
- **Per-contact vs Per-page**: Competing scrapers bill for every page scraped (e.g. 3.36 pages per domain), charging you whether a contact exists or not. Here you only pay when real data is found.

See the live rates on the [Pricing page](https://apify.com/sumitr_mardy/website-contact-finder/pricing).
